高斯消元

假设我们希望求解这么一个方程组：

$$ \left\{ \begin{aligned} a_{11}x_{1}+a_{12}x_2+...+a_{1n}x_{n}&=b_1 \\ a_{21}x_{1}+a_{22}x_2+...+a_{2n}x_{n}&=b_1 \\ ...... \\ a_{m1}x_{1}+a_{m2}x_2+...+a_{mn}x_{n}&=b_m \end{aligned} \right. $$

高斯消元

$$ \left\{ \begin{aligned} 2x+y-z&=8 \\ -3x-y+2z&=-11 \\ -2x+y+2z&=-3 \end{aligned} \right. $$

先用 (1) 式消元 (2) 式

$$ \left\{ \begin{aligned} 2x+y-z&=8 \\ 0x-\frac{1}{2}y+\frac{1}{2}z=1 \\ -2x+y+2z=-3 \end{aligned} \right. $$
