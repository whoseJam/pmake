三角计数

$$ \begin{aligned} answer&=\sum_{y=1}^n\sum_{l=1}^n\sum_{r=l}^n(2n-2) \\ &=(2n-2)\sum_{y=1}^n\sum_{l=1}^n\sum_{r=l}^n1 \\ &=(2n-2)(\sum_{y=1}^n1)(\sum_{l=1}^n\sum_{r=l}^n1) \\ &=(2n-2)n(\sum_{l=1}^n\sum_{r=l}^n1) \\ &=(2n-2)n(\sum_{l=1}^n(n-l+1)) \\ &=(2n-2)n(\sum_{t=1}^nt) \\ &=n^2(n+1)(n-1) \end{aligned} $$
